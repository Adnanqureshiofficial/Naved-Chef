## The Concept

Pain au Chocolat is a classic expression of French viennoiserie, built around laminated pastry and a rich chocolate centre.

The focus of the piece is simplicity, precision, and the quality of the laminated dough.

## Technique

The pastry requires careful control throughout the lamination process to create distinct layers of dough and butter.

Precise shaping and proofing are followed by controlled baking to develop a crisp exterior and a light, layered interior.

## Texture

The contrast between the crisp laminated exterior and the soft pastry surrounding the chocolate centre defines the experience of the finished product.

## Presentation

The finished pastry maintains the clean, recognisable form of classic pain au chocolat while showcasing the precision of traditional viennoiserie technique.
