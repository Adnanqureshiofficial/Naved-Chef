## The Concept

A dessert built around the contrast between rich chocolate and the delicate sweetness of poached pear.

The composition combines a smooth ganache with the soft texture and subtle fruit character of pear, creating a dessert that is indulgent without becoming overly heavy.

## Composition

The flexible ganache forms the rich centre of the dessert, providing a deep chocolate character and smooth mouthfeel.

Poached pear introduces freshness and natural sweetness, creating a complementary fruit element within the composition.

## Technique

The ganache is prepared to achieve a smooth and flexible texture suitable for controlled plating and clean presentation.

The pear is carefully prepared to retain its delicate texture while absorbing the flavours of the poaching process.

## Presentation

The dessert uses the contrast of dark chocolate and pale pear to create a refined visual composition with a focus on texture and simplicity.
