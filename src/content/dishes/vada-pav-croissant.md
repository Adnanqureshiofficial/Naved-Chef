## The Concept

Vada Pav Croissant is a contemporary interpretation of Mumbai's iconic street-food combination through the language of French viennoiserie.

The concept brings together a familiar local inspiration with the laminated structure and presentation of a croissant.

## Inspiration

The idea is rooted in the contrast between traditional Indian street-food flavours and classical pastry technique.

The result is intended to feel recognisable while presenting the inspiration in an unexpected form.

## Technique

The croissant begins with the fundamental principles of laminated dough — controlled fermentation, precise butter incorporation, careful lamination, and consistent shaping.

The final product requires attention to proofing and baking to achieve the characteristic layered structure of a well-made croissant.

## Presentation

The finished croissant combines the visual identity of classic viennoiserie with an unmistakably local inspiration.
