## The Concept

Independence Day is a sculptural chocolate piece created to translate the spirit of national celebration into a handcrafted pastry and chocolate form.

The design focuses on structure, proportion, detail, and the visual possibilities of tempered chocolate.

## Design

The showpiece is conceived as a centrepiece rather than an individual dessert, giving greater emphasis to height, silhouette, balance, and visual impact.

Every structural element contributes to the overall form and character of the piece.

## Chocolate Craft

Working with chocolate at showpiece scale requires careful control of tempering, thickness, assembly, and structural stability.

Each component is prepared and assembled with attention to precision so that the final sculpture remains both visually refined and structurally sound.

## Presentation

The completed piece is designed to command attention as a display centrepiece, combining craftsmanship with the celebratory character of the occasion.
