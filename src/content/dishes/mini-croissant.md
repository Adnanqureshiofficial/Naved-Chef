## The Concept

The Mini Croissant focuses on the precision required to translate classic croissant technique into a smaller format.

Working at a reduced scale makes consistency particularly important, from lamination and shaping through to proofing and baking.

## Technique

The process follows the essential principles of traditional laminated viennoiserie.

Careful control of dough temperature, butter distribution, lamination, shaping, and proofing helps preserve the characteristic layered structure at a smaller size.

## Texture

The finished pastry aims to retain the essential qualities of a classic croissant — a delicate exterior, defined layers, and a light interior.

## Presentation

The smaller format makes the pastry suitable for breakfast service, buffets, afternoon tea, and composed pastry presentations.
