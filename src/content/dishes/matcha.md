## The Concept

A contemporary dessert built around the distinctive character of matcha.

The composition explores the balance between matcha's natural bitterness and its delicate aromatic quality, creating a dessert with a clean and refined flavour profile.

## Flavour

Matcha provides the central identity of the dish, bringing earthy, vegetal, and gently bitter notes.

Supporting elements are carefully balanced to complement the intensity of the tea without masking its character.

## Technique

The preparation focuses on maintaining the freshness and aromatic quality of matcha while creating contrasting textures throughout the dessert.

Precision in portioning and plating ensures that the individual elements remain balanced.

## Presentation

A restrained presentation allows the natural colour of matcha to become part of the visual identity of the dish.
