## The Concept

Raspberry and pistachio form the foundation of this contemporary dessert, bringing together bright fruit character and the richness of roasted nuts.

The composition is designed around contrast — acidity and richness, softness and texture, colour and restraint.

## Composition

Raspberry provides a fresh and vibrant fruit profile, while pistachio contributes depth, nuttiness, and a naturally rich finish.

The individual components are arranged to create a balanced progression of flavour and texture.

## Technique

The dessert focuses on precise preparation of each component and careful control of texture.

Particular attention is given to maintaining the freshness of the raspberry while allowing the pistachio elements to retain their character.

## Presentation

A clean plated arrangement highlights the natural contrast between the raspberry and pistachio components, creating a refined contemporary finish.
