## The Concept

World Cup Passion Fruit is a vibrant plated dessert built around the distinctive tropical character of passion fruit.

The concept combines a bright, energetic flavour profile with a contemporary presentation, creating a dessert that feels both expressive and refined.

## Flavour

Passion fruit provides the central identity of the dessert, bringing natural acidity and tropical freshness to the composition.

The supporting elements are designed to complement the fruit rather than overpower it.

## Technique

The preparation focuses on precision, consistency, and maintaining the freshness and intensity of the passion fruit profile.

Each component is carefully finished before plating to create a clean and controlled presentation.

## Presentation

The final plating uses colour and contrast to create a visually expressive dessert while maintaining the clean aesthetic expected from a modern pastry presentation.
