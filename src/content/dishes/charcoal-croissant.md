## The Concept

The Charcoal Croissant explores the visual contrast between the deep tone of charcoal and the delicate layered structure of laminated pastry.

The focus is on combining a distinctive appearance with the craftsmanship and texture expected from classic viennoiserie.

## Technique

The croissant is built through controlled fermentation and lamination, with careful attention to dough development, butter distribution, shaping, and proofing.

Maintaining clean layers throughout the process is essential to achieving the final honeycomb structure.

## Texture

The finished croissant is designed around the contrast between a crisp, delicate exterior and a light, layered interior.

## Presentation

Its dark appearance creates a strong visual identity, making the croissant particularly effective as a contemporary pastry presentation.
