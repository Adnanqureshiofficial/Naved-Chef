## The Concept

A contemporary interpretation of the classic tiramisu, separating its familiar elements and rebuilding them as an individual plated dessert.

The dish preserves the identity of tiramisu while presenting its flavours and textures through a more modern visual language.

## Composition

Espresso-soaked ladyfinger provides the familiar coffee character, while mascarpone brings richness and softness.

A delicate cocoa tuile introduces a crisp textural contrast and reinforces the chocolate and cocoa notes associated with the classic dessert.

## Technique

The dessert relies on precise preparation of each individual component before being assembled on the plate.

The balance between creamy, soft, crisp, and coffee-infused elements is central to the final composition.

## Presentation

The components are intentionally separated and arranged with precision, allowing the guest to experience the familiar flavours of tiramisu through a contemporary presentation.
