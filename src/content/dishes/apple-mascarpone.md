## The Concept

Apple Mascarpone brings together the freshness of apple with the richness and smoothness of mascarpone.

The dessert is built around a simple flavour pairing, allowing the natural character of the apple and the creamy texture of mascarpone to define the composition.

## Composition

Apple provides freshness, acidity, and natural sweetness, while mascarpone contributes richness and a soft creamy texture.

The supporting components are designed to create textural contrast and maintain balance throughout the dessert.

## Technique

Careful preparation of the apple ensures that its texture and freshness remain present in the finished dish.

The mascarpone component is handled to maintain a smooth and delicate consistency suitable for plated presentation.

## Presentation

The final composition focuses on clean forms and balanced proportions, creating a contemporary expression of a familiar fruit-and-cream pairing.
