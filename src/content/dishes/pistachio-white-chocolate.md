## The Concept

Pistachio and white chocolate form the central pairing of this contemporary entremet.

The dessert is designed around the contrast between the naturally nutty character of pistachio and the creamy sweetness of white chocolate.

## Composition

Pistachio brings depth, nuttiness, and a distinctive roasted character, while white chocolate provides richness and sweetness.

The individual layers are designed to create a progression of flavour and texture from the first cut to the final bite.

## Technique

The entremet requires careful preparation and assembly to maintain clean layers, defined textures, and a precise final shape.

Attention to temperature, setting, and finishing is essential during the construction and final presentation.

## Finish

The final finish is kept refined and contemporary, allowing the pistachio and white chocolate pairing to remain the visual and flavour focus of the entremet.
