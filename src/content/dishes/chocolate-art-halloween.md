## The Concept

A Halloween-inspired chocolate art piece exploring the darker and more theatrical side of chocolate craftsmanship.

The design uses the sculptural qualities of chocolate to create a dramatic centrepiece with a strong seasonal identity.

## Design

The piece is developed around silhouette, depth, and visual contrast.

Rather than functioning as a dessert, the chocolate is treated as a medium for three-dimensional artistic expression.

## Technique

The construction requires careful tempering and handling of chocolate components, followed by precise assembly of the individual elements.

Structural balance is particularly important when working with larger chocolate forms.

## Presentation

The finished showpiece is intended to create an immediate visual impact, combining craftsmanship with the theatrical atmosphere associated with Halloween.
