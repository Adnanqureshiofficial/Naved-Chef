## The Concept

A refined interpretation of panna cotta, combining the richness of white chocolate with the clean, cultured character of yogurt.

The dessert is designed around balance — a delicate creamy texture, gentle sweetness, and a fresh finish that keeps the composition light and elegant.

## Composition

The white chocolate provides depth and richness, while yogurt brings acidity and freshness to the base.

The presentation focuses on clean forms and restrained elements, allowing the panna cotta to remain the central expression of the dish.

## Technique

Careful preparation of the panna cotta base is essential to achieve a smooth, delicate texture while maintaining enough structure for a refined plated presentation.

The final dessert is finished with attention to temperature, texture, and visual balance.

## Presentation

A minimal contemporary presentation allows the soft texture and subtle contrast of the dessert to take centre stage.
